Free Online Databases

Reference platforms and reading resources listed on the FEU Diliman Library Wix page.

Home/Resources/Free Online Databases

Tritech Digital Media

Tritech Digital Media is an online ebook reading platform. It enables users to read and download ebooks based on various genres such as agriculture sciences, life sciences, engineering and technology, social sciences, tourism and hotel management, general reference books, and others.

Directory of Open Access Journals

A database of the University of Lund, Sweden, which provides access to quality controlled scientific Open Access Journals covering several subject areas.

Cambridge University Press

Cambridge University Press works closely with the global academic community to publish high quality academic content and deliver tools and services that advance learning and research.

Vooks

Vooks brings children's stories to life with animated illustrations, read-along text, and narrated stories in an ad-free platform for classroom use.

JSTOR Open Access

Search open access content on JSTOR without a login, including more than 6,000 ebooks and over 150 journals.
