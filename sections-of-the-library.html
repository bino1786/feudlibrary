Library Sections

Sections of the library from the original FEU Diliman Library service page.

Home/Services/Library Sections

Circulation Section

Circulation section handles the releasing and returning of books for home use and room use.

Filipiniana Section

Books about the Philippines covering subject areas written by Filipino and foreign authors, published here and abroad, are found in this section. Undergraduate theses are also available.

Reference Section

Encyclopedias, atlases, dictionaries, handbooks, manuals, and special collections are available in this section. All materials are for room use only.

Internet Work Station

Computers with internet connection and electronic resources are found in this section. Users are given 15 to 20 minutes on a first come, first served basis.

Reading and Enrichment Area

Computers with internet connection and electronic resources are found in this section. Users are given 15 to 20 minutes on a first come, first served basis.
