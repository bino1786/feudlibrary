Our Library

A learning hub for Basic Education, SHS, and College communities.

The FEU Diliman Library is an indispensable educational repository. It aims to provide and support the instructional, curricular, and research needs of the students and associates.

The collection of books is classified under Circulation, Reserve, Reference, Filipiniana, and Periodicals sections. These can easily be accessed using the Online Public Access Catalog (OPAC).

The Electronic Library is also available to provide members of the academic community highly effective learning experiences and services through web and internet technologies as an extension of the conventional library.

Mission

The Mission of FEU Diliman Library aims to provide resources, instructional materials, and various programs to enhance students' information literacy skills to become socially responsible lifelong learners. It is committed to stimulate interest in reading that will support the Basic Education and Tertiary Curriculum and afford access to technology for the latest information.

Vision

The FEU Diliman Library envisions itself in supporting the institution's commitment by delivering competent library services and academic information resources for the Basic Education and Tertiary.
